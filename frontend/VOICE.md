# Voice guide

How Prompt Force talks to people (UX-DR18). Every story that adds UI text follows this.
All UI text is Norwegian bokmål; code and comments stay in English.

## Tone

- **Warm, calm and short.** One idea per sentence. The app motivates without shouting.
- **"Du"-form.** Speak directly to the person: "Her logger du det du spiser", not
  "Brukeren kan logge mat".
- **Sentence case** everywhere. No all-caps labels, no exclamation marks in a row.

## Buttons and links

- **Start with a verb** that says what happens: "Lagre måltid", "Legg til øvelse",
  "Bytt til mørk modus", "Godta og fortsett". Not "OK", "Send" or "Ja".
- One primary (plum) button per view.

## Words

- **Plain words, no jargon.** Say "dagens mål" and "kcal igjen", never BMR, TDEE,
  makros or other fitness terms without an explanation.
- Numbers in ink, with units: "1 640 kcal", "3 × 10".

## Errors and empty states

- **Start with what to fix:** "Skriv inn en e-postadresse." rather than "Ugyldig input".
- Do not blame: "Vi fikk ikke kontakt med serveren. Prøv igjen." not "Du gjorde noe feil".
- Empty states invite the next step: "Du har ikke logget noe i dag ennå. Legg til et måltid."

## Never

- **No streaks, badges, confetti or "Du har brutt rekken din".**
- **No shaming:** going over the calorie target is not an error, is never red and gets
  no warning tone. "Du er 200 kcal over målet i dag" is enough.
- No emoji in UI text or as icons.
